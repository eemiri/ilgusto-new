## Profile Summary

**Anna is a 32–48 year old woman living in Kleinblittersdorf, Saarbrücken, or the surrounding Saar region.** She plans most of the couple's or family's dining-out decisions. When she wants a proper dinner — not fast food, not a chain — she searches online, checks the website, and if it looks right, she calls to reserve. She has heard about Il Gusto from a colleague or friend. She is curious but needs confirmation before committing.

---

## Driving Forces

### ✅ Top 3 Positive Drivers (What She Wants)

**1. Confidence that this is the right choice**
- She raised expectations with her partner / family by suggesting Il Gusto
- She needs to feel certain before she picks up the phone
- Success = opening the website and immediately feeling "yes, this is it"
- **Il Gusto Promise:** Quality signal within 3 seconds of landing — photography, typography, and tone all confirm: this is fine dining.

**2. See the menu and understand the price range**
- She wants to know what kind of food is served and whether it fits the occasion
- "Authentic Italian" is too vague — she wants specifics (hausgemachte Pasta, Wolfsbarsch, Argentine Roastbeef)
- **Il Gusto Promise:** Full menu visible on the page, categorized, with prices, and at least one signature dish that creates desire.

**3. Easy way to reach the restaurant**
- She will call — not fill a complex form
- The phone number must be visible without hunting
- **Il Gusto Promise:** Phone number persistent in the header/footer, prominent reservation CTA in at least 2 sections.

### ❌ Top 3 Negative Drivers (What She Fears)

**1. The website not matching the actual quality**
- A generic Bootstrap template signals "cheap" even if the food is excellent
- She has been disappointed before by restaurants that looked better online
- **Il Gusto Answer:** The design itself communicates quality — Awwwards-level execution is proof before the food arrives.

**2. Not being able to book easily**
- If the phone number is buried or the reservation process unclear, she will move on
- **Il Gusto Answer:** Phone number in the nav, in the hero CTA, and in the footer.

**3. Showing up and feeling out of place**
- She wants to know the vibe: is it casual? formal? family-friendly?
- **Il Gusto Answer:** Gallery + About section shows real atmosphere — warm, elegant, intimate.

## What Anna Needs to See on the Page

1. **Hero:** Stunning food or interior shot + "Frisch. Elegant. Unvergesslich." + immediate call-to-action
2. **Menu:** Full, readable, with hausgemachte Pasta prominently featured
3. **Gallery:** Real restaurant photos — not stock
4. **About:** Warmth + story — who is behind Il Gusto
5. **Reservation:** Phone number always visible, hours clearly stated
