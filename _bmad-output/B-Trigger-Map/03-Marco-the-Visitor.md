**1. Authentic Italian — not a franchise**
- He can spot a generic pizza chain from a mile away
- He wants to see handmade pasta, real Italian dishes, a genuine Italian identity
- **Il Gusto Promise:** Hausgemachte Pasta as hero, Italian dish names, authentic brand voice.

**2. See the actual food**
- Gallery with real dish photography converts sceptical visitors
- **Il Gusto Promise:** Dish photos from the archive featured prominently.

**3. Feeling welcome as a first-timer**
- Copy that is warm and inviting, not cold or exclusive
- **Il Gusto Promise:** Tone of voice: "Erleben Sie..." — inviting, not gatekeeping.

### ❌ Top 3 Negative Drivers

**1. Choosing wrong and wasting a special evening**
- He has one night — he cannot afford a bad pick
- **Il Gusto Answer:** 4.9★ rating + review quote surfaced on the page builds instant trust.

**2. Not having a table**
- He may try to walk in without a reservation
- **Il Gusto Answer:** "Reservierung empfohlen" clearly stated + easy phone number.

**3. Language or cultural barrier**
- Especially relevant for French cross-border guests
- **Il Gusto Answer:** Clear, clean German copy. If French visitors are a priority, a simple "Réservation: +49..." note could be added.
