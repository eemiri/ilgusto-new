## Profile Summary

**Petra is a 35–60 year old organising a special occasion** — a birthday, anniversary, retirement dinner, or small corporate event. She is responsible for the success of the evening and cannot afford to get it wrong. She will research several venues and make a detailed comparison before contacting anyone. Il Gusto's 35–40 capacity makes it ideal for intimate group events.

## Driving Forces

### ✅ Top 3 Positive Drivers

**1. Confidence the restaurant can handle her group**
- She needs to know the capacity, the setup, and whether private events are welcome
- **Il Gusto Promise:** Reservation section mentions "Feiern Sie besondere Anlässe in modernem Ambiente mit bis zu 35–40 Personen."

**2. A venue that will impress her guests**
- The venue needs to look and feel special — not a banquet hall, not a chain
- **Il Gusto Promise:** The design itself demonstrates taste. A website this quality tells Petra the restaurant has standards.

**3. Easy, direct contact**
- She will call or email to discuss the specifics
- **Il Gusto Promise:** Phone + email both visible. Response feels personal, not automated.

### ❌ Top 3 Negative Drivers

**1. The restaurant being too small or unprepared for groups**
- She needs confirmation before investing time in an enquiry
- **Il Gusto Answer:** Group capacity (35–40) and event capability mentioned in the reservation section.

**2. Disappointing her guests**
- She is staking her reputation as the organiser
- **Il Gusto Answer:** 4.9★ + review testimonials reassure her she's making a safe, impressive choice.

**3. A frustrating booking experience**
- Multiple back-and-forth emails with no response = she moves on
- **Il Gusto Answer:** Phone number prominently featured. Email listed. Response expectation set.
